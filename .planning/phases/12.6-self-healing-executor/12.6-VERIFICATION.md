### Architectural Note on Plan 03 Debug Route Claim

Plan 03 stated that `src/api/routes/debug.ts` would `contain: "correctionModel"` and that self-healing context would be "passed from debug route to executor." The actual architecture is different but functionally equivalent:

- The debug route (debug.ts) does NOT call `executePlan` directly — it generates a plan and returns `skillName` + `containers` to the client in the response body.
- The execute route (execute.ts) reads `skillName` + `containers` from the POST body, looks up the skill via `deps.registry.get(skillName)`, and wires `correctionModel: deps.provider?.registry?.get?.('default')` into `ExecutionDeps`.
- The CLI (commands.ts) forwards `skillName` and `containers` from the debug response to the execute call.

This client-mediated chain fully satisfies the goal: self-healing IS activated for all DPEV flows. The plan's artifact claim was architecturally imprecise, but the end-to-end truth holds.
